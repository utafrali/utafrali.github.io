Sayfa — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Sayfa (com.utafrali.sayfa) handles your information. We respect your privacy and collect as little data as possible.

Data collected through advertising

Sayfa is free and supported by ads served through Google AdMob. To deliver ads, AdMob may collect:

  • Device identifiers (e.g. the advertising identifier / IDFA)
  • Advertising data (e.g. which ads you have been shown and interacted with)

This data may be used for third-party advertising and, with your permission, for tracking across apps and websites owned by other companies. You are asked for tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency) and can decline; you can also opt out via your device settings. Google's handling of this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy and AdMob's documentation.

In-app purchases

Purchases (such as removing ads) are processed by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment details.

Data we do NOT collect

We do not require an account, and we do not collect your name, email, contacts, or location for our own use. Gameplay/app data is stored on your device.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them.
