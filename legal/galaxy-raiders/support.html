Galaxy Raiders — Support

Need help with your planet, a raid, or a purchase? Email tafraliugur@gmail.com with your commander name and planet coordinates; we usually reply within two days.

Common questions

My purchase didn't arrive. Open the Store and tap Restore. If Plasma is still missing, email us with the date of purchase.

I changed phones. Sign in with the same Apple ID; your planet follows your account.

Delete my account. Email us and we will remove your account and planet.
