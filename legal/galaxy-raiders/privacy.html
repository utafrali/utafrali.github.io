Galaxy Raiders — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

This policy describes how Galaxy Raiders (com.tafrali.spacegame) handles your information. We collect only what the game needs to run its shared online galaxy.

Account (Sign in with Apple)

You sign in with Apple. We receive a stable Apple user identifier and, only if you choose to share them, your name and email (which may be an Apple private relay address). We use this solely to keep your planet attached to your account. We never see your Apple password.

Gameplay data

Your commander and planet names, coordinates, resources, buildings, fleet, raids and battle reports are stored on our game server (Supabase) because the galaxy is shared: other players can see your commander name, planet name, coordinates and level, and can raid your planet.

Push notifications

If you allow notifications, we store your device push token to warn you when your planet is under attack. You can turn this off at any time in Settings.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Free players see ads served by Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising identifier) and advertising data. You are asked for tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency) and can decline; ads are then non-personalized. See the Google Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases

Purchases (Plasma packs, Remove Ads) are processed by Apple. We receive a transaction record to credit your account; we never receive your payment details.

Deleting your data

Email us from the address below to have your account and planet deleted from our server.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal data.
